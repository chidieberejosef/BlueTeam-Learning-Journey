**Good Analysts Investigate "behaviour" and not "reputation" because an "attacker" can download a malware from "Onedrive" and when the IP is checked, It reveals its from Microsoft.**
- The service may be legitimate
* The activity might not be


**Never investigate only The IP:**



Investigate:
- the user
* the process
+ the destination
- the time
* the behavior
+ the sequence of events

This is called context.
**Context is one of the most valuable things a SOC analyst develops.**
