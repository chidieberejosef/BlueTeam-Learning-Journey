IP Address: Also known as Internet Protocol is a protocol that tells devices where a packet is coming from and where it is going. |
MAC Address: It is a unique identifier of every device |
DNS: it is a protocol that translate website names into their public Ip addresses for it to be located on the internet |
Gateway: As the name signifies, Its simply the entrance by which devices communicate to the internet |
Port: Is the endpoint by which communication or information reaches a device |
