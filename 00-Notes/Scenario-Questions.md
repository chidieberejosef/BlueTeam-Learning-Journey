# Scenario Questions

1. Why would you choose network segmentation as a mitigative effort when a finance PC was compromised in an organization?

Answer: The attacker's ability to move laterally is restricted by segmentation and firewall policies
Note: Segmentation doesn't stop attackers from compromising a host. It slows or prevents them from moving to other systems.
That time is incredibly valuable because it gives defenders a chance to detect and respond.
